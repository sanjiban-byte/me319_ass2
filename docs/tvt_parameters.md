# ME319 A2: parameters, data split (train / test / validation) and metrics

Prepared for Sanjiban (paper assembly), 15 Nov 2026. Section 1 is written by
hand; sections 2 onwards are generated by `evaluate_tvt.m` from the frozen code
in `ME319_A2_merged` (MATLAB). Decision numbers (Dxx) refer to the pipeline
document (`ME319_A2_Pipeline_and_Work_Split.md`, rev 9).

## 1. Parameters: given, set or tuned

**Labels.** *Given*: fixed by the assignment. *Set*: chosen by a stated
argument (physics, analysis or a convention) and never adjusted against
simulation scores. *Derived*: computed from other parameters by a formula.
*Tuned*: chosen by comparing simulation results; the data used is that
parameter's **train** set.

### 1.1 Train sets

| Name | Used to tune | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Train-E** (estimator) | estimator model and noise parameters, predictor order | Sanjiban's `estimator_tests/make_test_drones.m`: 12 drone types (straight, three weaves, jink, random, near_fast, far, and four blackout types) with an ideal matched turret. Sweeps 1-2: 30 runs per type, seed 1. Confirmation, also used in the final choice: 40 runs per type, seed 2. Predictor order: an earlier Python study, 8 types x 40 runs. |
| **Train-G** (gate and reset) | fire-gate form and threshold, post-blackout reset | `compare_designs.m`, 115 closed-loop cases: Scenarios A, B and C from 5 initial turret angles (-10, 10, 37.5, 60, 85 deg) = 15; seeded random weave cases 4-53 = 50; the same 50 with a forced full 0.5 s blackout (ids 1004-1053) = 50. Radar-noise seed 2044. |

The controller gains (T1) were **not** tuned on any scenario data: they come
from linear analysis (stability margins, damping, lag matching).

### 1.3 Turret plant and feedback loop (Jithu, T1)

| Parameter | Value | Label | Logic / data |
|---|---|---|---|
| V_max (drive voltage limit) | +/-600 V | Set | Not given in the assignment. Typical industrial servo DC bus (560-680 V); leaves a ~3x turret-rate margin over the fastest line of sight (D8). |
| Current rating (reported, not enforced) | 50 A | Set | Assumed drive rating; the measured peak is 35 A, during saturated first slews only (D22). |
| tau_stop (hard-stop model) | 5 ms | Set | Modelling choice: rigid, inelastic stop; small against all loop time constants. |
| K_v,fb (feedback velocity constant) | 8 1/s | Set (analysis) | Largest value that keeps robust margins at the 50 ms update: GM 11.0 dB, PM 61 deg; GM >= 13 dB with +/-20 % errors in J or L; K_v = 10 gives 16 % overshoot (D18). |
| K_P | 8012 V/rad | Derived | K_v,fb / K_vel, with K_vel = KtN/(bR + KtN KeN). |
| Notch zeros (omega_n, zeta_res) | 55.94 rad/s, 0.0936 | Derived | Equal to the plant's electromechanical pole pair, so the notch cancels it. |
| Notch poles damping zeta_d | 0.7 | Set | Standard well-damped choice. |
| Notch discretisation | pole-zero matched | Set (analysis) | Tustin goes unstable at 50 ms; matching keeps exact cancellation at any dt. |
| Integral action | none | Set (analysis) | A type-2 loop would remove the deliberate 0.5 s lag (D3). |
| dt_ctrl (internal update grid) | 50 ms | Set | Equal to the radar interval: no new information arrives faster (D20). |
| t_reset_jump | 0.5 s | Set | A backward time jump larger than any ODE-solver step back means a new run (D19). |

### 1.7 Data split used here

* **Train**: Train-E and Train-G above. Nothing in sections 2-4 was used to
  choose any parameter, and no parameter was changed after seeing them.
* **Test** (46 cases, 40 % of the 115 Train-G cases): three trajectory
  families not present in any train set:
  * **T1, multi-jink** (16): three coordinated turns at random times, 8-19.5 m/s^2, 0.3 s onset;
  * **T2, two-frequency weave** (15): two sinusoids per axis (0.3-2.5 rad/s), up to 19 m/s^2;
  * **T3, sustained coordinated turn** (15): constant turn rate from a random start time, 6-19 m/s^2.

  Starting range 1000-1900 m; speed 60-145 m/s; mostly crossing; 75 % have
  one blackout (onset 1.0-8.5 s, 0.1-0.5 s); initial turret angle uniform over
  -10 to 85 deg; radar seed 2044.
* **Validation** (16 designed scenarios x 10 radar-noise seeds = 160 runs), initial turret angle 37.5 deg:
  * **V1, near the distance and velocity limits**: a 150 m/s pass at 820 m (V1a) and at 1700 m (V1b); a 150 m/s orbit at 1950 m (V1c); a 148 m/s orbit at 1150 m, a = 19 m/s^2 (V1d).
  * **V2, sudden turns at full a_max** (19.8 m/s^2, 0.2 s onset): a turn at 3 s (V2a); the same turn starting exactly at blackout onset (V2b); a reversal from +a_max to -a_max inside a blackout (V2c); a turn at 1 s with a blackout over 1.0-1.5 s, during the first slew (V2d).
  * **V3, radar loss at different times and several losses in one run**, on a weaving target: one loss at 1.0 s (V3a) or 8.5 s (V3b); two losses (V3c); three losses (V3d).
  * **V4, very close and very far**: a weaving pass with closest approach 830 m (V4a, plus V4b with a blackout at closest approach); a weaving orbit at 1900-1960 m (V4c, plus V4d with a blackout).

  V3c, V3d (two or three losses) and V2c (a reversal inside a blackout) go
  beyond the assignment's stated limits on purpose (stress cases).

**Overlap note.** Validation families resemble some of Sanjiban's Train-E
drone types (jink, blackout_double, near_fast, far). The trajectories, the
generator and the closed-loop turret are different, so for the estimator
parameters V2-V4 are related-but-unseen rather than fully independent. They are
independent of Train-G.
