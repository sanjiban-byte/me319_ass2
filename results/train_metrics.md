## Estimator: bias (E1), calibration (E3), accuracy (E4)

e = r sin(theta_hat - theta), positive = estimate above the drone. E1 uses one mean per run (runs are independent; ticks inside a run are not). ## Estimator: error vs sideways acceleration (E2)
