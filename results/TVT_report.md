## 1. Parameters: given, set or tuned

**Labels.** *Given*: fixed by the assignment. *Set*: chosen by a stated
argument (physics, analysis or a convention) and never adjusted against
simulation scores. *Derived*: computed from other parameters by a formula.
*Tuned*: chosen by comparing simulation results; the data used is that
parameter's **train** set.

Sweeps 1-2: 30 runs per type, seed 1. Confirmation, also used in the final choice: 40 runs per type, seed 2. Predictor order: an earlier Python study, 8 types x 40 runs. |

Radar-noise seed 2044. |

from linear analysis (stability margins, damping, lag matching).

### 1.7 Data split used here

* **Train**: Train-E and Train-G above. Nothing in sections 2-4 was used to
  choose any parameter, and no parameter was changed after seeing them.
* **Test** (46 cases, 40 % of the 115 Train-G cases): three trajectory
  families not present in any train set:
  * **T1, multi-jink** (16): three coordinated turns at random times, 8-19.5 m/s^2, 0.3 s onset;
  * **T2, two-frequency weave** (15): two sinusoids per axis (0.3-2.5 rad/s), up to 19 m/s^2;
  * **T3, sustained coordinated turn** (15): constant turn rate from a random start time, 6-19 m/s^2.

  Starting range 1000-1900 m; speed 60-145 m/s; mostly crossing; 75 % have
  one blackout (onset 1.0-8.5 s, 0.1-0.5 s); initial turret angle uniform over
  -10 to 85 deg; radar seed 2044.
* **Validation** (16 designed scenarios x 10 radar-noise seeds = 160 runs), initial turret angle 37.5 deg:
  * **V1, near the distance and velocity limits**: a 150 m/s pass at 820 m (V1a) and at 1700 m (V1b); a 150 m/s orbit at 1950 m (V1c); a 148 m/s orbit at 1150 m, a = 19 m/s^2 (V1d).
  * **V2, sudden turns at full a_max** (19.8 m/s^2, 0.2 s onset): a turn at 3 s (V2a); the same turn starting exactly at blackout onset (V2b); a reversal from +a_max to -a_max inside a blackout (V2c); a turn at 1 s with a blackout over 1.0-1.5 s, during the first slew (V2d).
  * **V3, radar loss at different times and several losses in one run**, on a weaving target: one loss at 1.0 s (V3a) or 8.5 s (V3b); two losses (V3c); three losses (V3d).
  * **V4, very close and very far**: a weaving pass with closest approach 830 m (V4a, plus V4b with a blackout at closest approach); a weaving orbit at 1900-1960 m (V4c, plus V4d with a blackout).

  V3c, V3d (two or three losses) and V2c (a reversal inside a blackout) go
  beyond the assignment's stated limits on purpose (stress cases).

**Overlap note.** Validation families resemble some of Sanjiban's Train-E
drone types (jink, blackout_double, near_fast, far). The trajectories, the
generator and the closed-loop turret are different, so for the estimator
parameters V2-V4 are related-but-unseen rather than fully independent. They are
independent of Train-G.

## 4. Validation: estimator

Bias (E1, t-test across runs), error vs sideways acceleration (E2), calibration (E3) and accuracy (E4) per category: `results/val_metrics.md` (test set: `results/test_metrics.md`).

Per-tick t-statistic: at every 50 ms tick, t = mean / (sd / sqrt(10)) of the cross-range estimate error r(theta_hat - theta) across the 10 seeds (H0: zero mean, df = 9, two-sided 5 % critical value 2.262). If the estimator were unbiased at every tick, about 5 % of ticks would exceed it by chance. The trajectory is the same for every seed, so a deterministic lag (for example during a turn) shows up as large |t|.
